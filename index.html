About

Irene Becker Studio 2012.

After graduating with a degree in Fine Arts from the Complutense University of Madrid, I began working as a freelance designer and artist, developing graphic design, illustration and painting projects for over a decade.

In 2023, I expanded my creative practice by training as a Full Stack web developer at Codenotch (Madrid), incorporating web development into my work process.

Today, I work at the intersection of art, design and technology, creating visual identities and web experiences where the conceptual and the technical are part of the same creative process.

Approach

A website is no longer optional.

Today, a website is often the first real point of contact between a business and its audience.

It shapes perception, builds credibility and gives structure to everything a brand wants to communicate.

More than a digital presence, it is a space where identity, content and experience come together with clarity and intention.
